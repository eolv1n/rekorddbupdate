# Taste Calibration - 2026-08-15

This pass uses 50 tracks whose rekordbox content records were manually updated
on 2026-08-15. Their file creation and library-add dates vary, so the learning
signal is the current manual classification, not track freshness.

## Strong Signals

- Rating and Color are separate dimensions. Rating describes energy and set
  function; Color describes the sonic lane.
- Blue is not limited to low-energy material. Emotional, melodic, vocal, and
  trance-leaning tracks can be Blue at rating 3-4 and MAIN TIME.
- Purple can carry the progressive peak. Dark, acid, hypnotic, or psychedelic
  Progressive House can remain Purple at rating 4 with MAIN TIME/PEAK roles
  instead of becoming Red.
- Orange is not an energy shortcut. It can describe acid, indie, psychedelic,
  or club-break character at rating 2-3 and WARM/JOURNEY.
- Green remains the restrained organic/deep lane and can carry rating 2-3
  WARM/JOURNEY; it does not mean OPEN only.
- Breaks continue to split by mood: Aqua/Blue for deep and emotional material,
  Orange for acid/electro club pressure. Emotional Breaks can still reach
  rating 4 MAIN TIME while staying Blue.
- Yellow is used selectively for House/classic/piano club language, not as a
  generic color for energetic material.

## Calibration Examples

- Estiva - The Wave: Blue, rating 3, MAIN TIME; emotional/female-vocal
  Progressive/Trance with drive.
- Julian Nates - Echoes of Another Life: Purple, rating 4, MAIN TIME/PEAK;
  acid, dark, hypnotic, psychedelic Progressive House.
- Sebastien Leger, Lost Miracle - Mistily: Orange, rating 2, JOURNEY;
  acid/psychedelic Organic-Progressive character without peak energy.
- Fahlberg - The Shift: Orange, rating 3, WARM/JOURNEY; indie-leaning melodic
  material whose color does not imply MAIN TIME.
- Borowsky, Lilova, ETNE - Flower: Blue, rating 4, MAIN TIME; emotional
  female-vocal Breaks.
- Air Baxx - Firewood: Orange, rating 4, PEAK; acid/electro Breaks where the
  club-pressure meaning of Orange is explicit.

## Agent Consequence

The agent must infer Rating, Color, and Set Role independently, then check that
their combination makes dramatic sense. It should not use a fixed mapping such
as Blue equals rating 2, Orange equals rating 4, or Purple equals JOURNEY only.
