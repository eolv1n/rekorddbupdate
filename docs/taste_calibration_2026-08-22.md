## Main Lesson

Rating, Set Role, and Color are independent dimensions:

- Rating describes intensity.
- Set Role describes dramatic placement in a set.
- Color describes sonic character.

The agent must infer all three separately and then compare the combination with
current local examples. A rare combination is a confidence warning, not an
automatic error.

## Confirmed Patterns

- Red can occur at rating 2 with MAIN TIME or PEAK for dark, bass-heavy,
  hypnotic Melodic Techno and Indie Dance. Confirmed examples include
  Adriatique / GENESI - Closer, Glowal / Massano - Future Generation, and
  Volkoder / CamelPhat - Unique Moment.
- Blue carries emotional and melodic material across the full intensity range.
  OceanLab - Satellite is Blue at rating 5 MAIN TIME; Aly & Fila - We Control
  The Sunlight remains Blue at rating 4 MAIN TIME.
- Aqua is not restricted to warm or low-BPM Breaks. Jon Gurd / Reset Robot -
  Akura is Aqua at rating 3 PEAK because the high-BPM broken-beat lane remains
  emotional and melodic.
- Orange is a crossover character, not a MAIN-only color. It appears at rating
  2 WARM for JUNO / Alex Wann - Allo, rating 2 JOURNEY for GENESI - 94, and
  rating 4 MAIN for Dee Montero - Synthesia.
- Yellow is a stable House lane at several intensities and roles: Hot Since 82
  - Wow is rating 2 WARM, Silva Bumpa - On 2nite is rating 3 MAIN, and Mark
  Knight / Cristoph - Yebisah is rating 4 MAIN.
- Purple spans hypnotic Progressive, warm progressive Breaks, emotional Trance,
  and journey Psy-Trance. It should not be tied to one energy level or role.
- Green remains organic or restrained Progressive, including rating 3 WARM for
  M.O.S. - One Love.
- Future House metadata is not decisive. GENESI - 94 is Orange JOURNEY, while
  Maddix - Acid For Breakfast is functionally Red peak Techno despite the source
  genre label.

## Review Policy Change

Low Rating with MAIN/PEAK is no longer a blanket discrepancy. Review it only
when the dark, bass, acid, hypnotic, melodic-techno, or indie lane is absent.
Likewise, rating 5 outside Red/Orange is valid for strongly emotional Trance or
Breaks examples.
